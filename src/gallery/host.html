sample-1. A stand-in for the dashboard's view of one plugin. This page draws the heading and the tab row, and frames the plugin's page seamlessly: the page drops its own, and the frame takes the viewport's height under this page's chrome, the page's height budget. The dot after the plugin's name is the page's status, drawn here from its meridian:status. The buttons left of the level switch are the page's header actions, drawn here from its meridian:actions (an icon where the kit knows one, its words the button's name and tooltip); pressing one sends meridian:action back, and the page presses its own. The level switch is always the rightmost; on a phone it is a menu naming the level.
